📚 What is the Study Planner?

My Study Planner is a website designed to help students organize their study time and keep track of important assignment deadlines.

The planner allows students to view their weekly study schedule, check upcoming assignments, and manage their time more effectively.

✨ Features

  • 📅 Weekly study schedule
  • 📝 Assignment due-date reminders
  • ⏰ Priority levels for assignments
  • 📊 Quick overview of upcoming work
  • 📚 Study tips and organization
